Entry for the make.fun competition
[news-image-20261119](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119):
sum up 19 November 2026's world news in one image.

The image is a newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November 2026.
Its four panels cover the day's biggest stories: Grand Theft Auto VI
begins its global rollout after a 13-year wait; the US–Iran exchange
of fire widens across the Gulf; House Democrats nominate Hakeem
Jeffries for Speaker; and Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east
coast. The footer strip carries the rest of the day: the EU's €45
billion Ukraine loan, the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in
Shenzhen, Nvidia's board ruling out Jensen Huang's return, Fed
minutes pointing to further rate hikes, Voyager 1 reaching one
light-day from Earth, and Pew's projection that Islam becomes the
world's largest religion by 2035.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
into the output directory as `entry.png`.
